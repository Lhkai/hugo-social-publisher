Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

1. Overview

Hugo Social Publisher is a personal-use social media content management application designed to organize and publish original content to authorized social media accounts.

2. Information We Process

Depending on the permissions granted, the application may process account identifiers, profile information, authorization tokens, video content, captions and publishing status.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is used to authenticate connected accounts, manage content and perform publishing actions authorized by the account owner.

4. Data Storage and Security

Access credentials and authorization tokens should be stored securely and must not be exposed in public repositories. Access is restricted to the application operator.

5. Data Sharing

Information may be transmitted to connected social media platforms as necessary to perform authorized publishing operations. The application is not intended to sell personal information.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

The account owner may revoke platform authorization and request deletion of locally stored application data. Data deletion requests may be submitted through the contact method provided by the application operator.

7. Third-Party Platforms

Connected platforms operate under their own privacy policies and terms of service.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated when application features or data practices change.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact the application operator using the contact information published on the application's official website.
